How We Rank Providers

Rankings are based entirely on publicly available data. Each provider earns a composite score from four signals. Ranking position is never influenced by payment.

  • {{ weights.price }}%
    Price competitivenessPrices at or below the ${{ stats.median|intcomma }} {{ location.city }} median score highest; the further above the median, the lower the score.
  • {{ weights.breadth }}%
    Procedures listedProviders listing more procedures tend to be more established practices, which contributes to a higher score (capped so no single provider dominates).
