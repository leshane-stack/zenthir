What Affects Botox Cost in {{ location.city }}?

Across these {{ provider_count }} providers, Botox in {{ location.city }} ranges ${{ stats.min|intcomma }}–${{ stats.max|intcomma }} ({{ stats.range_multiplier }}x). Here's what drives the difference.

Units vs. flat rate

Botox is priced per unit ($10–$20) or per area/treatment ($250–$600); a full face runs 40–60 units.

By treatment area

Typical amounts: forehead 10–20 units, crow's feet 12–24, glabella (frown lines) 20–30, full face 40–60, lip flip 4–6.

Brand

Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin give similar results at different per-unit rates. Dysport is often cheaper per unit but uses more units, so compare the all-in price.

Provider type

Med spas typically run 10–20% cheaper than plastic surgery practices, with dermatology in between.

What's included

Prices usually cover product plus injection; consultation ($50–$150), touch-ups, and gratuity are often extra, so confirm the all-in price before booking.

How long it lasts

Results last 3–4 months, so annual upkeep is about 3–4 treatments, roughly ${{ annual_low|intcomma }} to ${{ annual_high|intcomma }} per year at the {{ location.city }} median.
