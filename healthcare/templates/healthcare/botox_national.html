What Affects Botox Cost

The national median is ${{ stats.median|intcomma }} per full-face treatment — here's what moves the price.

Units vs. flat rate: Botox is priced per unit ($10–$20/unit) or per area ($250–$600/area). A full face typically needs 40–60 units.

Treatment area: forehead 10–20 units, crow's feet 12–24, glabella 20–30, full face 40–60, lip flip 4–6.

Brand: Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin have different unit equivalencies but similar results; Dysport is often cheaper per unit.

Provider type: med spas run ~10–20% cheaper than plastic surgery practices.

How long it lasts: results last 3–4 months, so annual upkeep is roughly 3–4x the per-treatment price (about ${{ annual_low|intcomma }}–${{ annual_high|intcomma }}/year at the national median).
