Before You Book

Questions to ask before booking Botox in {{ city }}.

  • How many units are included in this price?
  • Is this Botox Cosmetic, Dysport, Xeomin, or another brand?
  • Who performs the injection: an MD, NP, PA, or aesthetician?
  • Are touch-ups included if results are uneven?
  • Is the consultation fee separate or included?
  • What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

How to Compare Botox Prices

Don't compare prices without asking what's included. A provider at $399 and one at $599 may not be comparable if one includes 50 units and the other 30, or if one uses Botox Cosmetic and the other uses Dysport, which needs more units for an equivalent result. Two quotes are only comparable once you match them on the same basis. Always compare total units, the brand used, the injector's credentials, and the touch-up policy.
