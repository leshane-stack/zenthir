What Affects Dental Implant Cost in {{ location.city }}?

Across these {{ provider_count }} dental offices, a single implant in {{ location.city }} ranges ${{ stats.min|intcomma }} to ${{ stats.max|intcomma }} ({{ stats.range_multiplier }}x). Here's what drives the difference.

What the price includes

A single-implant quote may cover just the implant post, or the complete tooth (post + abutment + crown). Always confirm which — a post-only price looks cheaper but isn't comparable to an all-in quote.

Abutment and crown

The abutment (connector) and the crown (visible tooth) are often billed separately from the implant post and can add $1,000–$2,500 combined, depending on crown material (zirconia, porcelain-fused-to-metal).

Implant brand

Premium implant systems (Straumann, Nobel Biocare) cost more than value brands; the difference reflects the fixture, not necessarily the outcome for a straightforward case.

Bone grafting and sinus lift

If the jaw lacks enough bone, a graft or sinus lift is needed before or during placement, commonly adding $400–$3,000. Not every patient needs one.

Sedation and anesthesia

Local anesthetic is usually included; IV sedation or general anesthesia is an add-on, often $250–$1,000, and more common at oral surgery practices.

Oral surgeon vs general dentist

General dentists and specialists (oral surgeons, periodontists) both place implants. Specialists may price higher and are often chosen for complex cases, grafting, or medical complexity.
