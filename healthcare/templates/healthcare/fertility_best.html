How We Rank Providers

Rankings are based entirely on publicly available data. Each provider earns a composite score from four signals. Ranking position is never influenced by payment.

  • {{ weights.price }}%
    Price competitivenessPrices at or below the ${{ stats.median|intcomma }} {{ location.city }} median score highest; the further above, the lower the score.
  • {{ weights.breadth }}%
    Procedures listedProviders listing more procedures tend to be more established practices (capped so no single provider dominates).
  • {{ weights.verified }}%
    Verification statusProviders that have claimed or verified their profile rank higher. Claiming is free and does not buy position.
  • {{ weights.type }}%
    Provider-type relevanceFertility clinics and hospital fertility programs (the settings that perform these procedures) rank ahead of unrelated types.
