Why {{ procedure.name }} Prices Vary

{{ procedure.name }} costs range from ${{ procedure.cost_range_low|floatformat:0 }} to ${{ procedure.cost_range_high|floatformat:0 }} depending on several factors:

Facility type is the biggest driver. Hospitals charge significantly more than outpatient surgery centers or independent imaging facilities due to facility fees and overhead costs. The same MRI scan can cost $300 at a standalone imaging center and $2,500 at a hospital.

Geographic location affects pricing. Providers in New York, San Francisco, and Boston typically charge 20-45% more than providers in the South and Midwest for identical procedures.

Insurance vs cash pay creates different price tiers. Cash prices are often lower than the listed "chargemaster" rate. Many providers offer cash pay discounts of 30-60% for patients paying out of pocket.

Provider reputation and quality ratings also influence pricing. Higher-rated facilities and board-certified specialists tend to charge more, though higher price doesn't always mean higher quality.
